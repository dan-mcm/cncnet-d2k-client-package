Thufir Hawat

Hawat is Duke Leto's mentat. Hawat is one of the best mentats in the known universe, and besides his amazing computer-like mind, he is also very skilled in combat, proving that mentats can also be good warriors. Thufir is extremely loyal to House Atreides. In fact, for three generations, Hawat had been responsible for Atreides House security. This meant protection for Atreides family members, anti-espionage, risk management, and various aspects of kanly. This man is one of the backbone elements of House Atreides.
