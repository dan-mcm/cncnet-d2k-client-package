House Ginaz

House Ginaz is a relatively strong House, member of the Landsraad council. They are reputed for their swordmaster schools. It is said Ginaz-trained warriors are one of the best in the entire known universe. House Ginaz has a long-standing feud with House Moritani, over which the Moritani have had several important victories over the years. The relatively recent alliance with House Atreides has allowed House Ginaz to prosper, as the Moritani did not want to declare war again due to Atreides intervention in the war.
