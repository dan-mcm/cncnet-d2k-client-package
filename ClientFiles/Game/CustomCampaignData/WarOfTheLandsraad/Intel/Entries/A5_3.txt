House Richese

House Richese is the direct competitor of the Ixians regarding technology. Although they do offer a considerable amount of useful machines, they are totally outmatched by House Vernius. This is the main reason why there is a long-lasting feud between these two Houses. House Richese's diplomacy is pretty unstable, as they had pacts and agreements with a lot of Houses, including Atreides, Harkonnen, Ordos and Corrino. There was even a Harkonnen-Richese direct alliance, which, however, did not last long.
