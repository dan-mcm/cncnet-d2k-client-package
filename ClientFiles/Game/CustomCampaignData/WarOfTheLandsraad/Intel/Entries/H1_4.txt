"Kill them all, boys!"

Extracted from a Harkonnen soldier war journal: "I didn't really care that much about this. It was the baron hating the Atreides so much, I, personally, had no issue, I mean... I wasn't gonna earn anything out of it. But I must admit, when general Gato Radnor shouted through the station: <Kill them all, boys!>, there was an amazing feeling that got over my entire body. We were going to strike men who were not expecting it. Backstabbing felt so good... I was worried that I was also turning into a ruthless brute."
