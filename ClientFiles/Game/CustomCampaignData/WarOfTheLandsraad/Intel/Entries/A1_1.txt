Gurney Halleck

Halleck is one of the best warriors in the known universe, and a brilliant military strategist. He is also the most loyal friend of Duke Leto Atreides, and a fearsome enemy for the foes of House Atreides. It is said that no riches in the known universe can buy the loyalty of good Gurney. Halleck is not only skilled in battle, but he is also a talented minstrel who is skilled in the use of the baliset. He would often play music on this instrument to entertain his guests, or to fill the dining halls of House Atreides with music when he was in their service. "He could be killing you while he was singing and never miss a note." - Duncan Idaho.

Gurney was born, ironically, on Giedi Prime. After an incident with local militia, in which his sister was killed, he went off-planet to seek refuge from the Harkonnen. In the War of the Landsraad timeline, Gurney moved directly to Caladan, and started serving Duke Leto Atreides I. Gurney's hatred for the Harkonnen, combined with his military assets, makes him an amazing commander to fight House Harkonnen.
