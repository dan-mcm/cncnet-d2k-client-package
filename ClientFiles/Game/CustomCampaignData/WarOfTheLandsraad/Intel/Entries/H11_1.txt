The Siege of Harko City

Although not the brightest military operation in history, Rabban's siege of Harko City was a notable move. The execution was not outstanding, but not bad either. However, on one side you got a guy with almost no real military skills, and on the other, a battered veteran general. Nonetheless, the siege and assault of Harko City was a bloody battle that would cost House Harkonnen heavy manpower and resources.
