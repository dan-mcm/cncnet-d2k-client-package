House Mutelli

House Mutelli is a relatively mediocre member of the Landsraad in terms of influence and power. While their own achievements are not worth being noted in any history books, the Mutelli have had a pretty stable position thanks to the relations they have built with House Harkonnen and House Hagal, although the latter would directly side with the Corrinos on one particular matter, ending the good diplomatic relations between the Mutelli and House Hagal. The Mutelli joined House Harkonnen in the invasion of Caladan to gain favors with House Harkonnen and to increase their own influence within the Landsraad. A House who invaded Caladan would certainly become more popular and more feared.
