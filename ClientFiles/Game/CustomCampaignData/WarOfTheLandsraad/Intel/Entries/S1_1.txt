Burseg Zed Varlaz

One of the highest ranking Sardaukar commanders. He has participated in multiple important campaigns for emperor Shaddam IV, and has been one of the most important and successful bursegs on Arrakis during the First Spice War. Varlaz is reputed to be a honourable man, bound by the Sardaukar code to serve his emperor or empress until death. Varlaz's brilliant military tactics were not enough to stop the advance of the Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos coalition, however, during the siege of the Imperial Palace in the Imperial Basin. Varlaz lived one of the most painful experiences for a Sardaukar: the collapse of the Imperial Palace where his emperor was located.
