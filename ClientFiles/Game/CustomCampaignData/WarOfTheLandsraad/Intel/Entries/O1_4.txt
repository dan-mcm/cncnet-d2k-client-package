Ordos emergency procedures: P-V4

Whenever the Ordos are going through hard times, there is a certain emergency procedure than can be enacted by the Executrix. P-V4 stands for Preservation-Ordos version 4, and is one of the nine preservation directives that the Executrix can enact if needed. The procedures have a clear target: saving money and assets. To achieve this, the Ordos must severely reduce the budgets of certain areas. The 4 variant of these procedures refers to the reduction of off-planet war budget, but the preservation of the Executors, mentats and military personnel wages to avoid desertion. Other variants would cut these too, but would only be chosen under very strict and dire situations.
