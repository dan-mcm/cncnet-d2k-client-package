"Friends with the Sardaukar?" - Atreides captain war journal

"I got some weird orders today from my general. <Don't fire upon the Sardaukar.> I wasn't sure what was going on. We used to fight the Imperial elite forces for a while. Initially I thought this would not last for long, but then... I found myself fighting side by side the Imperial Sardaukar. All I can say is... it felt good to have those elite troops next to you."
