The Return to Kaitain - Varlaz's Journal

"I wasn't sure it's real. To see the beautiful Kaitain and Corrinth City after such a long period. Period full of war, blood, death and pain. The peaceful Corrinth City was the perfect paradise for a war commander. Too bad we were not there on vacation: the princess must be kept safe. We were expecting a possible invasion of our beautiful Kaitain. The princess always looked like she had everything under control, but we were not so sure about it at that time..."
