The Tleilaxu suspicions

While the wars on Caladan and Ix were in progress, it seems princess Irulan Corrino and House Hagal would not sit idle. Considerable amounts of funding were invested into getting Imperial spies on Draconis IV and Tleilax, to fight out why did the Ordos and Tleilaxu join House Richese in the ixian invasion. While the spies did not manage to find the real reason, some precious information was, in fact, gathered, and considerable suspicions began to form in regards of the Tleilaxu.
