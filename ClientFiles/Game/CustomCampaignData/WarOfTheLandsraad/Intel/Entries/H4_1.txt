Feyd-Rautha Harkonnen

Feyd is the youngest son of Baron Vladimir Harkonnen. Even though he's younger than Rabban, Feyd is much more capable and is more mature. He is a skilled military strategist, and has definitely inherited his father's talent for plots. While his brother is called a "brute" due to his sheer aggressive attitude and thirst for blood, Feyd is more calculated, and would sometimes show mercy for those who fail him just to he can use them again for his own reasons. Feyd is also a "ladies man", being extremely handsome and always preoccupied by having beautiful girls around him.
