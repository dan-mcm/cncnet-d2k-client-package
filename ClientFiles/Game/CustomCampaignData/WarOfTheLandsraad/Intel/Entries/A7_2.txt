Serena's Necklace

The Caladian islands, called Serena's Necklace, are an important strategic point for the Atreides, as they shelter their famous wingboat factories. Besides, they are also used for Atreides military naval production of Cruisers, Destroyers and Gunboats. The islands were one of the primary Harkonnen targets during the Caladan invasion, in order to decrease the Atreides naval capabilities and to be able to hijack Atreides ships so that Harkonnen forces can actually cover their ground troops with Navy, while fighting the Atreides Navy too.
