Gurney Halleck's relations with the Arrakis Smugglers

Gurney is a highly respected man along the Dune Smuggler organizations. This is mostly due to his charming baliset songs, his friendly attitude and also due to Leto's strategy of making friends with different factions on Dune who are not direct competitors, such as the Smugglers and the Fremen. Gurney is considered, by some of the organizations, a brother, and this is something that the Atreides have taken advantage of quite a few times during the First Spice War.
