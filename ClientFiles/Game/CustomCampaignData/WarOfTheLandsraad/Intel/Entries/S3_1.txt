Arrakian Smuggler organizations

Not a lot is known about the Dune smugglers, except the fact that they organize into multiple organizations, relatively independent from each other. Each organization has a chosen leader, based on certain criterias which are unknown by most. The Smugglers are either in contact with bribed Guild navigators, Ixian special heighliners, or have aquired their own heighliners and are using them to either smuggle forbidden technology, transport troops and equipment between planets for costs much lower than the Guild's or transport mined spice towards their buyers off-planet. Before the First Spice War, the Smugglers would often hire mercenary companies to protect their installations from Fremen natives or other threats, including other smuggler organizations.
