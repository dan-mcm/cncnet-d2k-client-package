Mentat Lora Itami

This relatively young lady should not be underestimated. Lora is one of the most efficient and intelligent mentats in the known universe. She has a dominant attitude and can sometimes be the real brain behind all of Tyle's performances. Former partner of bounty hunter Norman Tyle, she joined the Ordos together with Tyle, running away from the Imperial manhunters. Lora is a very attractive woman, which is the reason while there seems to be more involved between Tyle and Lora other than just business. They are trying to hide their old friendship as much as possible, so that they can then talk freely, without being spied upon, about certain secret information or plots. Lora would often share classified information with Tyle, information that no Executor should be aware of. They always managed to do this secretly, and not gather too much suspicion from the Executrix.
