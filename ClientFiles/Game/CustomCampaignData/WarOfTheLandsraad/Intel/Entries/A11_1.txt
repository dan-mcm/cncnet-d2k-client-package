Mutelli turned on their former allies

Suspicious incidents over a Guild heighliner taking off from Kaitain towards Caladan, containing numerous Mutelli reinforcements, have caused heavy casualties for House Mutelli. The Atreides plot was successful, as Mutelli investigations would end up interpreting the events as a clear sign that House Harkonnen is sending an ultimatum to the Mutelli to stop their hostilities towards House Moritani, otherwise they will side with the latter. House Mutelli would, as a result, turn their weapons against Houses Harkonnen and Moritani.
