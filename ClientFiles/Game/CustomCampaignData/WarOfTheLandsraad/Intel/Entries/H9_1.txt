"And I was eager to fight him..."

Extracted from Gato Radnor's private journal: "Ever since I heared how he made Rabban look like a complete fool by destroying most of the Harkonnen forces on the southern continent in a matter of a few days, I was eager to fight Gurney Halleck. I thought I would finally get my chance close to Horizon, but the Baron had different orders. What is going on over there on Giedi Prime? Who knows... All I know is that I have lost my opportunity to defeat that Atreides hero who got so popular. <He took back most of Caladan by himself>, they say. Screw them, I say. And I was eager to fight him..."
