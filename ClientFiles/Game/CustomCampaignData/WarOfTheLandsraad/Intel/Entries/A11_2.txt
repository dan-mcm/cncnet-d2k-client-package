Sardaukar intervention on Caladan

The most powerful and influencial move in the entire war was, without a doubt, the Corrino intervention in the invasion of Caladan. Princess Irulan Corrino would send her Sardaukar legions to aid Houses Atreides and Ginaz. The combined Sardaukar, Atreides and Ginaz forces would prove to be a shocking hit to an already fractured invading coalition.
