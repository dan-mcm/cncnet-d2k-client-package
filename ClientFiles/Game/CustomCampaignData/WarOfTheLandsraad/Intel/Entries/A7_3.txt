Retaking Serena's Necklace

Although the Harkonnen and their allies managed to capture the islands for a period of time and create somewhat of a small naval fleet of their own out of the Atreides Naval Yards, it wasn't enough to stop the heavy Atreides naval retaliation. While the hijacked Harkonnen fleet fought well, the sheer size and strenght of the Atreides ships was too much, and soon, Atreides Destroyers and Cruisers managed to reach the islands and start a bombardment.
