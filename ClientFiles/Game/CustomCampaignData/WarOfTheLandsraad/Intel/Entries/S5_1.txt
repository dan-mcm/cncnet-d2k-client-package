Princess Irulan Corrino

As the eldest of five daughters to Shaddam, Irulan was raised under auspicious circumstances in the Imperial Court, and received an excellent education through the Bene Gesserit. Like her younger sisters Chalice, Wensicia, Josifa, and Rugi, Irulan was conditioned to be a lady of refinement and elegance.

Her talents have been observed ever since she was young. She has inherited some of her father's political traits, and is able to understand and manipulate the politics of the Landsraad. Although the daughter of Shaddam IV, Irulan did not agree with her father's plot regarding the First Spice War, and often tried to convince him to not get involved in the spice competition he created himself. Irulan is leaning towards a very powerful diplomatic style, in which she aims for a vast set of relations and alliances, rather than the pure and brutal military might of the Sardaukars.
