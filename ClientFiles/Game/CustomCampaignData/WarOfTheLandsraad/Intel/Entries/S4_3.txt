The fall of the Corrino-Guild alliance

During the First Spice War, emperor Shaddam IV has made an alliance with the Spacing Guild to overtake Dune from the three warring Houses. With considerable war attrition affecting them, Houses Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos were forced to form a coalition against the Corrino-Guild alliance. After many bloody battles, the coalition forces managed to push inside the Imperial Basin and kill Shaddam. After the fall of Shaddam, remaining Sardaukar forces were scattered, yet some of them managed to reunite under the command of Supreme Bashar Garron. The Sardaukar marshall did not share his presumably-dead monarch's view regarding the Guild, and did not try to maintain the alliance Shaddam created. Slowly, but surely, the surviving Sardaukar would consider the scattered Guild forces as enemy as well.
