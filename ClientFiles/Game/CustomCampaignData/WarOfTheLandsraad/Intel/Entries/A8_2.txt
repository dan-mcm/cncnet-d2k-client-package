"A blessing in the skies!" - Atreides captain war journal

"We had a very hard time dealing with all the enemy invaders. I was right there, doing my best, following general Halleck's strict orders... Attacks were closing in from all angles, we were running from one side of the base to the other, it was absolute chaos. Then, a blessing in the skies! About eighteen Ornithopters, raining death upon the enemy attackers. Lovely Harkonnen scum screaming in the fire of the bombardment!"
