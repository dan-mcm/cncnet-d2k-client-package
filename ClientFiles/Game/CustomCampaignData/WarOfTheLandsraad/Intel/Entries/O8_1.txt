Shaddam IV ghola

Bene Tleilax have aquired DNA samples from emperor Shaddam IV. It is unknown how exactly did they manage to do that, but it is certain that it was done while he was still alive. The Shaddam ghola is a Tleilaxu project, in which they are trying to create a ghola of the dead former emperor. House Ordos has initially showed interest in purchasing this ghola for their own plots, but eventually the Tleilaxu decided to use it themselves.
