The Great Siege of Draconis IV

The siege of the Ordos capital was called, in a generic way, the Siege of Draconis IV, although the name refers to the planet itself. Somehow it is true, since Tleilaxu forces are attacking all over the planet. Anyway, the assault on the Ordos capital was a costly battle, for both Ordos and Tleilaxu. The Ordos lost tremendeous amounts of assets, stored spice, structures and infrastructure, while the Tleilaxu lost the whole planet.
