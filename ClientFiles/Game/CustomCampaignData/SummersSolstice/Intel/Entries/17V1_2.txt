RPG Quad - LAV
     Where Summers' Stealth Raiders will no doubt help her against Moriaen's various superweapons, you have need of a more hardy high-end LAV. When the enemy stealth units must reveal themselves to attack, they'll butt heads with your RPG Quads.

     These uncommon LAVs are most often seen at the beginning of a battle, if at all, for their scouting proficiency. Their role in combat is usually filled by two different units, Grenadiers and standard rocket Quads, but there is much to appreciate about the combination of the two. The RPG Quad has the longest range of any LAV by far. It can effectively defend your base against any threat and contribute to the siege of an enemy base. Though more expensive, RPG Quads are no tougher than normal Quads.
