Summers' Light Combat Tank - Armor
     Combat Tanks are well-rounded units that comprise the core of any army. The light model used by the smugglers has weak armor, but excellent haste in both motion and firing. It can outpace other models of Combat Tank, but should beware longer engagements and battles of attrition it can't win. Positioning mistakes with light CTs are most punishing; they're easy to destroy. You also can't neglect anti-infantry weapons just because these vehicles can crush infantry. The act often damages the vehicle.

     Your MBTs, or main battle tanks, are suitable for frontal assault despite their weak armor. They can be paired with Quads for even better results. The Combat Tanks provide much-needed damage absorption while the Quads offer higher damage output.
