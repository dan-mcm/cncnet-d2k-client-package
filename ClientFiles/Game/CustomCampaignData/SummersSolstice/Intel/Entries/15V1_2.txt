Starport - Support Structure
     Although your forces control many Starports across Arrakis, you remain dependent on the Spacing Guild for interstellar travel. Unfortunately, Achen Moriaen was in the perfect position to cut you off from most of your technology. You've almost rectified that problem; the final step is to secure off-world reinforcements from a Guild Heighliner.

     The Ordos have assured you that their agents can discreetly send you units as soon as a Starport is under your control. Moriaen will likely destroy them once they're discovered, but the Starport can at least be used to complete this mission.
