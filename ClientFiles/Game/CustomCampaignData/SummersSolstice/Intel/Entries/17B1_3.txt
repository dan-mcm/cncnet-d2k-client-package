Grenadier - Infantry
     Quick support infantry ideal for dealing area damage to enemies. They are also your best asset for revealing hidden units, like Stealth Raiders or Fedaykin Gholas, when commanded to force-fire on their suspected path. To use the force-fire command, select a Grenadier, then hold CTRL and LEFT CLICK on where you would like them to attack.

     Stealth units can be prevented from re-cloaking by keeping fire on them. Autogun Turrets obviously would excel at this.

     You need an upgraded Barracks and an Outpost to deploy Grenadiers.
