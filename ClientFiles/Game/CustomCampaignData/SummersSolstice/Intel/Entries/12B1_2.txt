Tleilaxu Medium Combat Tank - Armor
     The Tleilaxu can't survive using only their living abominations. They devote some machines towards their malignant ends.

     Medium Combat Tanks are well-rounded units in motion, firepower, and armor. They are perfectly suited to assaulting fortified positions, covering friendly units, and quickly responding to attacks. However, they don't stand out in any one area.

     These units are also favored by the Ix as main battle tanks.
