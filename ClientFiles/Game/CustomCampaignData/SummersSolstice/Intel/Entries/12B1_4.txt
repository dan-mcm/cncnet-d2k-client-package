Axlotl Tank - Base Structure
     For all intents and purposes, Axlotl Tanks, also known as Flesh Vats, serve as the Tleilaxu equivalent of a Barracks. Gholas are created and conditioned within Axlotl Tanks. Even the structures themselves may live and breathe. Whatever the case, it's well known that genetic information can be coded into those bred in Axlotl Tanks. Therefore, capturing one would reveal who exactly the Tleilaxu are trying to revive, and perhaps why as well.

     The Ix are diametrically opposed to the Bene Tleilax by the natures of your respective ideologies. Because it's vital to defend yourself against them on occasion, your Engineers know how to manipulate some Tleilaxu technology, like Axlotl Tanks.
