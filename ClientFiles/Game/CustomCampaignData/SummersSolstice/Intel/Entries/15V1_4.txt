Storm Lasher - Base Defense
     Preliminary reports suggest this is an advanced stationary defense structure. It gives off enormous energy signatures, indicating a high power cost to function and potentially devastating, long range attacks. Moriaen has clearly been hiding this terrifying weapon until the appropriate time to spring it on you. One can only wonder what other secrets she holds...

     This structure is expected to behave differently from standard base defenses. Atani urges you to gather more information about these Storm Lasher megaturrets during the battle. If they have a weakness, you must find it!
