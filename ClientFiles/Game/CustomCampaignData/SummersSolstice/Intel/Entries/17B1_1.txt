Rifleman - Infantry
     Due to advancements in armor strength, traditional handheld rapid fire weapons have long been retired in favor of superior burst damage. The high-powered shotguns commonly used now have few disadvantages.

     When the need for higher range or quicker target re-acquisition arises, your basic infantry can be equipped with burstfire rifles instead. Although significantly less effective, especially against armored units, the superior range and rate of fire make Riflemen preferable for certain operations. This is rare, as Trikes, Raiders, and Autogun Tanks or turrets are readily available and capable of carrying similar guns heavy enough to be generally useful.
