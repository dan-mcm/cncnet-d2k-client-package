Sonic Tank - LRA
     The ultimate siege weapon. Sonic Tanks are weapons of Ixian design based on Weirding Modules, mounted atop a lightly armored chassis conceived by the Atreides. They are extremely effective against any ground target, but may require alignment to be used to maximum effectiveness as they annihilate targets in a straight line from their location. Many of these awesome weapons were left behind when House Atreides lost the war. Most have been claimed by House Ordos and its allies.

     These towering vehicles are only effective at range. Their tall cannons cannot rotate downwards to damage targets very nearby the treads, so they are vulnerable to aggressive advances. They can also be easily overwhelmed if surrounded.
