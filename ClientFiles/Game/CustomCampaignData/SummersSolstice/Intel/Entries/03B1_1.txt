Fremen Warrior - Infantry
     Desert natives. You are the descendents of Zensunni Wanderers who have adapted to the incredibly harsh planet of Arrakis. With water being such a rare commodity on the planet, your culture revolves around its preservation and conservation. All Fremen are capable of completely recovering their health even away from home, using secret survival techniques taught only by Dune.

     Fremen Warriors are vastly superior to other infantry and can even match Sardaukar legionnaires in combat. Imperial forces have the significant advantage of their organized, well-equipped military. Fremen are extremely distrustful of outsiders and tend to be openly hostile, though this attitude varies between Sietches.
