Issam Nehara - Commander
     Naib of Sietch Mushtamal. Your Fremen are gladly isolated in the howling crevices of the winding, windswept ridge. Though armed with very little, you, yourself, are a learned tactician and a master of stealth. You can personally guarantee their safety.

     The appearance of the visitor bodes poorly for your quiet home. You can expect to be accosted and terrorized by Imperial forces in pursuit of Summers as long as she remains in the territory. It's best that you escort her swiftly to the nearby smuggler base so the Imperials move on as well. You are a proud people and you will not be driven from your home.
