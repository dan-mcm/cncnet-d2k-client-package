Ornithopter - Special
     Small transport vessels and light attack craft capable of flight through jet propulsion, and navigation through their wings that mimic the movement of birds'. The Atreides' magnificent air fleet once dominated the skies of Dune, and the cornerstone of that fleet was the Ornithopter. You may be able to capture some Ornithopters for use against Moriaen in this battle.

     Many Ornithopters have fallen into the clutches of Achen Moriaen, who will use them to exterminate your ground forces as quickly as they can be readied and sortied. Their heavy presence in Tsimpo Heights demands the mass deployment of Missile Tanks and Rocket Turrets, their only deterrents. Airstrikes won't stop coming until Moriaen's HTFs or related Outposts are razed.
