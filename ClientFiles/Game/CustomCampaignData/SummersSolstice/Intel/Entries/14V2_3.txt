Dark Alliance Light Combat Tank - Armor
     Your cover is blown and of no further use. The time has come to cleanse Arrakis of the heathens. While your foes fight mostly among themselves for the moment, you must focus on the only one that recognizes you as the true threat. Many pockets of Ordos loyalists have been eliminated during the initial attacks. Some have overcome and regrouped. They must be finished off.

     The light Combat Tank sacrifices armor for movement speed and firing rate. While not ideal for frontal assault, its particular balance of attributes makes it the perfect main battle tank for swiftly routing your scattered and infighting enemies. You have enough medium and heavy Combat Tanks as well to put to use where and when they're most needed.
