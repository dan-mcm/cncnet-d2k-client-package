Death Hand Missile - Special
     A fairly precise warhead launched from the Palace of Destruction that annihilates all but the toughest targets. When a Death Hand is launched, you will be alerted. It's recommended you evacuate forces from the missile's suspected destination, if possible.

     These weapons were most often used by the Harkonnen during Emperor Corrino's gambit, but many of them were captured by Achen Moriaen following the defeat of the Harkonnen and their abandonment of Arrakis. They cannot be stopped through conventional means; you must prevent them from ever launching by destroying the Palace of Destruction itself. Moriaen's entire stockpile of Death Hand Missiles, Ornithopters, munitions and reserves in this region must be wiped out!
