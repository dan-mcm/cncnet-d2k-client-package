Kyne Giraud - Commander
     The fanatical Imperial Burseg. He commands the most ruthless army in the Known Universe in service to Emperor Corrino, but strength is only a footnote in his many qualities. The Sardaukar are known for their skills in espionage. He is a cunning foe. His targets rarely remain standing for very long once he finds and exploits their weaknesses.

     Proud and powerful, Giraud will do anything to accomplish his goals, and there are few things he cannot accomplish. Only one as clever as he, one well-equipped, and perhaps lucky as well, stands any chance.
