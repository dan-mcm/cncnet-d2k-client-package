OSO Heavy Factory - Production Structure
     Whereas Duelist Tanks were formerly your ideal armored assault weapon, your new Combat Tanks now fill that role. Deviators have also been deemed obsolete for typical OSO missions. Thus, the OSO Heavy Factory isn't equipped to build either of these.

     The deployment of much stronger Combat Tanks along with the ability to produce Missile Tanks will more than account for the absence of Duelist Tanks and Deviators. Your Heavy Factory retains the capacity to produce Autogun Tanks, which can even further bolster your frontline and serve as your ultimate armor-class unit.
