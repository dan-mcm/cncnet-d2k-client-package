Sardaukar - Infantry
     Emperor Corrino's fearsome and innumerable soldier-fanatics. Their abilities can be attributed to the harsh environment and brutal discipline they were exposed to on Salusa Secundus, where almost half died before the age of eleven. Each Sardaukar legionnaire is a formidable swordsman, skillful at espionage, and religiously devoted to the Golden Lion Throne.

     Common Sardaukar carry powerful handheld weaponry and low-yield explosives to damage vehicles in the event they're crushed, to resist the enemy even in death. Sardaukar can take the most punishment of any infantry on Dune, but they are still infantry. Large numbers of Riflemen, Grenadiers, Trikes or Raiders, Siege Tanks, or any vehicle that can crush infantry will suffice.
