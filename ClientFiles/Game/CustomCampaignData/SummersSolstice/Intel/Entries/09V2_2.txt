Tyrant Tank - Armor
     This colossal vehicle is a symbol of the Emperor's supremacy. It is infamously durable, and just as powerful. The massive cannon mounted to the frame can obliterate any target on the ground quickly. It's possible that Ordos Deviators were intended to counter these units when the time came, although unlike Harkonnen Devastators, they can't be ordered to self-destruct.

     These juggernauts are very rare, yet Emperor Corrino is said to be guarded by several of them. Sabre Durant is known to be in possession of one Tyrant Tank, probably for personal use. However, his penchant for heavy weapons and armor is tempered by strong pragmatism; he's waited until now to deploy it to Alazor Steppe.
