Carryall - Logistics
     Carryall pilots have one of the most dangerous jobs on Dune. A couple of rockets can destroy them and they're often lost in sandstorms. They bring reinforcements to battle, ferry Harvesters to and from Spice fields, and return vehicles to Repair Pads.

     Carryalls can facilitate long-distance harvesting, but it may be a better idea to expand near fresh Spice fields.
