Sumadi's Light Combat Tank - Armor
     Tough vehicles with rotating cannons. Sumadi's Combat Tanks can absorb damage for your weaker vehicles, but only so much. They trade armor for speed so they can outpace Imperial Combat Tanks, but perform poorly by comparison in longer engagements. If these units are sent into battle, your LAVs shouldn't be far behind.

     Combat Tanks aren't merely good frontline units on the attack. If you must draw out entrenched enemy forces, they also provide good cover for your infantry when positioned near the rocks they've garrisoned. This solid formation can be further strengthened by keeping LAVs ready to swarm and overwhelm approaching enemies.
