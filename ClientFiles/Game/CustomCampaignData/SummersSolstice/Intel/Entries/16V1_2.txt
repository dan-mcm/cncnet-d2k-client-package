Sardaukar - Infantry
     The formidable legionnaires of House Corrino. They are trained under impossibly harsh conditions and formerly held firm control of Arrakis with ease. Only the native Fremen Warriors can stand a chance against them in combat.

     Although the remnants of the Emperor's army are ruled only by chaos now, and have you in part to thank for that, they may put aside their hatred of you to exact revenge on the greater threat to the Known Universe - Achen Moriaen.

     The Imperial commander in this territory counts those mighty Sardaukar Elites and Tyrant Tanks among his forces.
