Siege Tank - LRA
     A howitzer designed to annihilate infantry and infrastructure alike. Siege Tanks are fairly cumbersome and can be destroyed easily by Quads and Combat Tanks. They should be kept behind frontline units or fortifications for the best results. Once your local Heavy Factory is upgraded and a High Tech Factory is built, Siege Tanks can be manufactured. More can be ordered at a Starport.

     Kailea Aurelianus is now aware of the situation and has agreed to configure your Heavy Factory to build Sonic Tanks. Although they require more upgrades and technology than Siege Tanks, they should prove useful for trashing everything on this deck once unlocked. They're only vulnerable up close; you can body-block enemy units from getting close with your heavy Combat Tanks.
