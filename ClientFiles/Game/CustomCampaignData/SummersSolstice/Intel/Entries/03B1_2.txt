Trike - LAV
     Fast light vehicles armed with mounted machine guns. They are suitable for scouting the desert and killing infantry.

     Although the Fremen lack an organized military, you make use of certain technologies for communication, travel, and hunting. Inexpensive Trikes are widely used across Dune, including by many Fremen. They're inferior to their more expensive and adaptable counterpart, the Raider, which is produced in place of Trikes by certain parties.
