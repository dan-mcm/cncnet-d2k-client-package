Sabre Durant - Commander
     The resilient mercenary commander. He's a pragmatic and dependable ally, with one unfortunate exception in the past. His tough forces are directly responsible for the supremacy of House Ordos on Arrakis, but his greatest loyalty lies with you.

     It's critical to your continued survival that you and Durant work closely together until every last one of the traitors is slain.

     Durant is known to possess a modified Duelist Tank and even an Imperial Tyrant Tank. Only his power armor may be available at the moment, without operable factories. It would be prudent to coordinate with him and his forces as needed during this battle.
