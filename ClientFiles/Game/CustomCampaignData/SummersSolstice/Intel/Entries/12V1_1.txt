Saboteur - Special
     Fearless, quick, stealthy infantry deployed from the Palace of Deceit. Armed only with explosives, Saboteurs are living bombs that can instantly destroy any structure they manage to infiltrate. They can also detonate near units. They have the potential to cause a tremendous amount of damage to amassed enemies, but you should be ready to follow up with LAVs and finish the job.

     Saboteurs sprint faster than any other infantry on the field thanks to cybernetic enhancements. Although Saboteurs with active camouflage can be detected by turrets or infantry in close proximity, they are at least guaranteed to get close to something before being executed. Use long range, heavy armor, or sacrificial Shotgunners against enemy Saboteurs.
