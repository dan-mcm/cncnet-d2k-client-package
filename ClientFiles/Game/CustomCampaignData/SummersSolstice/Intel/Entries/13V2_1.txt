Trooper - Infantry
     Fierce frontliners that annihilate vehicles and structures with armor piercing rockets. They make the perfect complement to Shotgunners for bursting down any targets in close range. They could also deter aggressive advances on paired Grenadiers.

     Although the powerful gauss rifles used by rank-and-file Sardaukar Troopers are best in the hands of a ghola, you may equip Troopers with the weapons if you wish. Gauss Troopers are stationed in all of your base structures for the purpose of defense.
