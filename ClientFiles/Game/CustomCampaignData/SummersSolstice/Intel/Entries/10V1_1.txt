Trooper - Infantry
     Tough soldiers that usually carry rocket launchers meant for the front lines against armored units. They're a great deterrent against LAVs when scattered on infantry rock, but Combat Tanks and LRA can outrange them. Enemy infantry are also a threat.

     Shotgunners accompanying Troopers provide a numerical advantage, draw fire, and help against common threats to Troopers.

     Ordos SpecOps infantry are trained to be secretive and ruthlessly efficient. With your personnel and House Ordos working formally together from now on, the forces you command are properly equipped, trained, and further bolstered by Ordos reserves.
