Sardaukar Ghola - Infantry
     Many Sardaukar fell at the battle of Shield Wall. If the Tleilaxu are here, it can mean only one thing: They are harvesting corpses to create Sardaukar Gholas. They are wretched Tleilaxu imitations of true Sardaukar legionnaires.

     Where the Ix represent mechanical innovation and technological superiority, the Tleilaxu worship flesh and blood. Gholas of any sort are created in Axlotl Tanks using the cells of dead individuals. The abhorrent things retain the memories of their former selves and are just as dangerous, but can receive additional programming, such as loyalty to the Tleilaxu.
