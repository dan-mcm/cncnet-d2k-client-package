Rowe Summers - Commander
     The resourceful ex-mercenary commander. While she shows weakness in caring for those under her command, she is remarkably obstinate. She is said to have joined the smugglers in pursuit of a less exciting career, an endeavor that ironically is resulting in more trouble for her as the Imperials hunt smuggler forces with greater ferocity in recent days. She is a confident hands-on commander with a track record of personally overseeing most operations from the battlefield itself.

     Summers' power armor is an Ixian-modified Sardaukar Elite suit. She cannot be suppressed or crushed, is highly resistant to damage, and regenerates to full health. Her modular minigun is similarly upgraded. Despite this, she'll need your help to survive!
