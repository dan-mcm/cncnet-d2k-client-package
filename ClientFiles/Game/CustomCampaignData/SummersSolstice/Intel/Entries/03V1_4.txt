Radnor Krillys - Commander
     The ruthless Harkonnen commander. The war-mongering Harkonnen are industrious and tough. While it's impossible to find love for them, their malevolence and brutality are nevertheless admirable. They're a force to be reckoned with even by themselves.

     The Harkonnen represent an imminent threat to any mining operations in or nearby their territories. You lack the strength to oppose them, despite possessing talented commanders like Summers. Although more likely to associate with you than the Atreides, any sort of alliance with the Harkonnen would result in intimidation and extortion. You will be attacked by them, but retreating to the shadows of Arrakis with losses would be far better than sacrificing your agency to their whims.
