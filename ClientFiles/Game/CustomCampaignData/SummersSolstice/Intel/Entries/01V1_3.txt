Sandworm - Native
     Ravenous predators indigenous to Arrakis that eat anything on the sand and create the Spice Melange. Although infantry are morsels much too small for their taste, vehicles on the sand will inevitably attract sandworms. Because Harvesters are always on sand while collecting Spice, they are most often the victims of sandworm attacks. You must watch over them carefully!

     In the event of a wormsign, retreat to rocky terrain and use explosive weapons like rockets or grenades to temporarily put the sandworm to sleep. It helps to garrison the armed infantry atop rocks nearby areas your Harvesters are active. You can also use Thumper infantry, which are commonly stationed at mining sites, to distract sandworms by deploying them where appropriate.
