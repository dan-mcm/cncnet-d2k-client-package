Kyne Giraud - Commander
     The fanatical Imperial Burseg. He commands the most ruthless army in the Known Universe in service to Emperor Corrino, but strength is only a footnote in his many qualities. His efforts in espionage and sabotage throughout the war have left the Imperials poised to retake Arrakis from the three great armies who fought among themselves. Still, he failed to account for one little thing...

     He commands his forces from the Command Center in northwest Alazor Steppe. The Atreides and Harkonnen supreme commanders are present there to coordinate for this battle. There has never been a greater opportunity to quickly end this war.
