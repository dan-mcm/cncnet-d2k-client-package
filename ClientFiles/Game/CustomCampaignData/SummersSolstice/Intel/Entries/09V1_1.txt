Sardaukar - Infantry
     Emperor Corrino's fearsome and innumerable soldier-fanatics. Sardaukar legionnaires are armed with various weapons, each very potent: Heavy burstfire rifles, burstfire miniguns, or gauss rifles. And, they can take the most punishment of any infantry on Dune. Sardaukar are religiously devoted to the Golden Lion Throne and will carry out the Emperor's will to the very end.

     Anti-infantry weapons like shotguns, machine guns, grenades, howitzers, or autoguns should be used against Sardaukar legionnaires of any sort. Infantry often damage vehicles when crushed, and your Combat Tanks have other battles to fight. You also happen to be in possession of an Ixian-modified Tyrant Tank, which'll prove great for combating more than just Sardaukar.
