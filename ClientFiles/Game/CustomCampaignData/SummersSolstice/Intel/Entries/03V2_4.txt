Autogun Turret - Base Defense
     Rotating dual guns best fortified behind Concrete Walls and supported by friendly forces. Autogun Turrets are cheap and sturdy, and can be rapidly erected in defense of a base. Like Concrete Walls, Autogun Turrets will block most projectiles.

     Durant's mercenaries have built some Autogun Turrets here in Bled al-Hazrad. It'd be wise to rally defenders around them.

     While effective at suppressing most enemy infantry and LAVs, their utter immobility leaves them vulnerable to a concentrated attack. And, their power can be cut by destroying enough Wind Traps, although Autogun Turrets are very power-efficient.
