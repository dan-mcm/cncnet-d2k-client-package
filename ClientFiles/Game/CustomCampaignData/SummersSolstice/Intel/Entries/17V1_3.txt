Flame Tank - LAV
     A shock-and-awe weapon your Heavy Factory is now configured to produce. The Flame Tank is built to quickly seize territory and clear enemy infantry and infrastructure in a blaze of glory. Its mounted flamethrower can project a continuous stream of fire onto specific nearby targets, rapidly incinerating one after another. It's also bulky enough to crush infantry.

     Commander Galletti warns against using Flame Tanks as frontline units. Unlike other tanks, they're vulnerable to grenades and howitzers, besides rockets. While your main force pushes into a base, Flame Tanks can raze undefended structures behind them.
