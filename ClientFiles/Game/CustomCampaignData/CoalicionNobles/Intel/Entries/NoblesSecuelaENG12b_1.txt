Invasion from the East:
The last base of the Tleilaxu can be attacked from various angles. The Lassoki decided that deploying their base to the East was the option that most benefited them strategically.

*Pros: Area very rich in resources, with the Mining Rigs located very close to each other without the need to expand.

*Cons: Very difficult to build a base due to the little space available to deploy structures.
