Bromeli turret:
It is the ultimate technology developed by the Bromeli. Loaded with a concentrated version of the Death Hand Missile, its damage output is overwhelming and it has a considerable firing range with 100% accuracy. All this added to a decent resistance this is without a doubt the most powerful turret ever created.

The only weak point is that they need a large amount of energy to function; without enough Windtraps the Turrets will be useless. It is possible to distinguish with the naked eye if the Turrets have enough energy or not.
