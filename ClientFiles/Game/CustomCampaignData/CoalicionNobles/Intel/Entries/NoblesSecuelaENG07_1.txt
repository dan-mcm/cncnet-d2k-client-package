House Bromeli:
The Bromeli have always been obsessed with being part of any House related to Technology, so much so that they invented the Saboteur, although they could not sell it because of the Ixians. They now have Ornithopters and the Death Hand Missile at their disposal to destroy their enemies, which, thanks to the experience gained during the war against the great Houses, they have learned to use more efficiently.

The Bromeli still lack an army of their own, which they will buy at the Starport, although this is a slow process as it is their only weak point.
