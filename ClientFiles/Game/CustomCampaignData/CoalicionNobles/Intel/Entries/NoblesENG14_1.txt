Unlimited Spice:
The Arrakis SandWorms are respected and admired in equal measure for being natural producers of the Spice Melange: the most valuable resource in the entire known universe. An area with multiple Sandworms will have the spice to sustain a battle for several hours.

The Emperor has several locked Worms under his control that are producing Spice indefinitely; no matter how much spice is harvested, it will never run out. The method used to create this SandWorm farm and how it is able to keep them away from the Harvesters while they do their work is unknown.
