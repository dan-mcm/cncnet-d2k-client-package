Enhanced Raider:
While this Ixian Raider produced by the Qaii has the same properties as the Raider built by the Lassoki, the Ixian gear based on the Bene Gesserit's "voice" causes them to do more damage than traditional machine guns.

Thanks to this they can even face heavy vehicles. However, they are still fragile against Siege Tank shells. In addition, its higher price makes Quads and Trikes still useful in battle.
