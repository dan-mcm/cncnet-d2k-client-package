Imperial Law:
After a brutal encounter between two rival Houses that ended in a massacre on the part of both sides, the Landsraad demanded that action be taken. The Emperor's response was the creation of Law 30-A, which specifies that, although armed conflicts are allowed on board the Ships of the Guild of Navigators, it is considered barbaric and inhuman to crush the infantry under the bearings of Heavy vehicles.

Although this is only a political maneuver to win the favor of the Landsraad, the Emperor himself has benefited from this law by being able to use his Sardaukar in any situation knowing that no one would commit the folly of trying to crush one of them, if not penalty of large fines or absolute exile.
