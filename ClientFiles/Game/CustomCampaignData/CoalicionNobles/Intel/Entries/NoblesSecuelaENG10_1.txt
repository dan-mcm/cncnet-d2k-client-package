Seneca:
Home planet of the Lassoki. An arid place with very few resources, it was very expensive for the Lassoki to start the invasion in Dune. Seneca was one of the planets controlled by "the league of nobles", from which the Lassoki were offered as a place to live.

After gaining control over Dune, the Lassoki used the income from the Spice Melange to study their own planet and discovered that Seneca was a planet rich in gems, buried underground.
