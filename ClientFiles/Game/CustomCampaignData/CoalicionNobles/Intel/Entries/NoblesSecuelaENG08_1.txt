House Qaii:
Isolated from much of the known universe, the Qaii were rarely inspected by the Emperor or any other influential group, which they took advantage of to create their own mechanized army, fast but weak due to the lack of quality materials.

The Qaii's specialty is Fast Vehicles, so their most efficient army is made up of Trikes, Quads, and Raiders equipped with Ixian weapons that they achieved during the course of the War against the great Houses.
