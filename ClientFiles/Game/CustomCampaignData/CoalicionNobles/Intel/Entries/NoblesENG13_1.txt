Airstrike:
Ornithopters are light flying machines capable of being equipped with bombs or missiles, but due to their fragile structure they are often destroyed on the way or even when already above enemy defenses.

The Bromeli, however, were cunning enough to add countermeasures in the form of small flames that will deflect the only weapon capable of destroying them: guided missiles. Thanks to this ruse, the Bromeli Ornithopter that has been successfully shot down remains to be seen.
