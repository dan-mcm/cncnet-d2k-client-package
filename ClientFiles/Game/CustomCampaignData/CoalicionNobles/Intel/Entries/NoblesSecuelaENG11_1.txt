Lassoki Capital:
Due to the fact that for many years the resources of the Lassoki were very scarce, they were never able to build a defensive system with energy shields or lasers, so they opted for poorer alternatives.

The Castle in which the Lassoki leader is located is formed by walls on top of mountains and surrounded by a mud moat that prevents invaders from passing through. Only now that the Lassoki have started mining for valuable gems have they been able to build defensive turrets.
