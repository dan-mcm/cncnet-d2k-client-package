Paying the Smugglers:
After the Coriolis storm, the Smugglers' mining base has been seriously damaged and they have lost all their Harvesters, so they urgently need money to be able to leave the territory and find another more profitable area. To do this they have put all that is left of their base up for sale for a price of 3 Spice Harvesters.

3 Harvesters must have their tanks completely filled with Spice to accept the payment (You will lose control of all 3 harvesters) and receive full control of what remains of the Smuggler base in return.
