Enhanced Raider:
The Bene Gesserit are known to use "the voice", a power that allows them to control weak-minded people or even injure them both physically and psychically. For years the Ixians tried to replicate that power in the form of a physical weapon.

When they finally succeeded, they equipped the Raiders with this weapon. The high rate of fire of this lightweight vehicle makes them more deadly than the Quad and, of course, much faster and more maneuverable than the Soldiers.
