Bromeli Against Ix:
The Ixians have the approval of the Coalition, since thanks to the new arsenal that they sold to their members, they were able to fight better against the three Great Houses. However, the Bromeli never got to use any of their technology during that war.

Furthermore, their jealousy against a group of inventors better than themselves has caused the Bromeli to want to cause damage against IX, and no better time to do so than now that they are developing a new technology.
