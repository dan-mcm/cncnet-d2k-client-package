Gold:
Caladan has always been considered a resource-rich planet, and this becomes true when finding veins of Gold everywhere with ease. It is possible for Harvesters to collect this precious metal and turn it into ingots at the Refinery.

It is a very quick resource to sell, so that the obtaining of money is practically instantaneous from the moment in which the Gold is converted into ingots. It is worth 700 credits per full Harvester.
