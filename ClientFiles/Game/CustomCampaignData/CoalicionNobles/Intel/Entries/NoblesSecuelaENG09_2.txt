Modified Raider:
The Qaii's new vehicle is a new Raider model capable of firing electrical charges that can cause major failures against any vehicle or structure. Since the shock does not affect Infantry, the Qaii have decided to keep the Raider's machine gun used in the previous model in order to be able to defend against any Infantry enemy.

Thanks to the previous battle, building one of these vehicles is expensive and time consuming, so the Qaii will only make limited use of these units during battle.
