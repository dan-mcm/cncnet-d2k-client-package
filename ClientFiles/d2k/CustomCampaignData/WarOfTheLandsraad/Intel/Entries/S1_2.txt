Supreme Bashar Cando Garron

As the rank suggests, Garron can be considered the marshall of the Sardaukar army. He has direct command over Zed Varlaz, and is the most experienced and decorated Sardaukar general who has ever served emperor Shaddam Corrino IV. Just like any Sardaukar, he is bound by honour to serve and fight for this emperor, until death. It is safe to say that Garron's multiple outstanding performances allowed the Sardaukar to get such a quick push over the already established Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos forces, when the Imperial legions joined the war on Arrakis.
