Executor Norman Tyle

Tyle is the most experienced Ordos Executor, and recently led most of the Arrakis operations. The rank of Executor is equivalent to the rank of a commander and a general for other Houses. The Ordos make no clear distinction between these two ranks, calling them Executors. Tyle is a former bounty hunter, and his most impressive achievement was killing a Sardaukar right on their training planet, Salusa Secundus. During his time as a bounty hunter, he met Lora Itami, with whom he has developed both a business relationship and maybe something more. After killing the Sardaukar soldier, Tyle and Lora were forced to run from the Imperial forces hunting them, and have found refuge working for House Ordos. Their identities have been altered, and the secrecy of the Ordos helped them stay off the Imperial radars. After the death of Shaddam IV, the Imperial threat above their heads has mostly dissipated.
