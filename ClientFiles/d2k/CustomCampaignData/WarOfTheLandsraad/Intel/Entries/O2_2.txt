Tyle and Lora's sharing of classified intel

Both Tyle and Lora would often share information that the other was not supposed to know. Although the Executrix would sometimes suspect this, the amazing performances of these two, combined with low risk of an actual problem because of them sharing knowledge was the reason the Executrix never pushed more into actually finding out how much secret intel they share. However, the Executrix was definitely not aware that the level of the information was sometimes critical, as otherwise they would have interfered and got rid of the two long ago.
