Gato Radnor

Radnor is the pure definition of ruthless Harkonnen efficiency. A man with a briliat military mindset, and extremely efficient tactics, Gato Radnor is one of the best commanders in the known universe. He has served the Baron in the First Spice War, which is where his outstanding performances advanced him towards the general rank very quickly. He survived a plot of the former Harkonnen mentat. Gato is a wise and calculated man, taking advantage of any situation. He had a rough childhood, with his entire family captured and mutated by Tleilaxu bio-engineers. Radnor's hatred towards the Tleilaxu is much stronger than the one for House Atreides.
