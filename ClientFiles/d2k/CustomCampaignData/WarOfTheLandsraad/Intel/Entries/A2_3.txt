Duncan Idaho's Journal - Harkonnen Betrayal

"I kept having that feeling... the feeling that something is about to happen. I told Gurney back in Arrakeen: <Hey, Shaddam is dead, the Guild is spread thin all over the planet... We won. Allied with the Harkonnen and Ordos. This cannot last for much longer>. Well it didn't. We woke up in the morning with tons of desperate screams. The screams of death. The nearby Harkonnen camp turned on us, and attacked us while sleeping. Most of my men were killed, and I barely managed to escape. I had a Sardaukar uniform and Sardaukar weapons, and I used them the best I could. I had to fight 9 Harkonnen soldiers by myself. This is what you get for trusting the Harkonnen, even one bit."
