Corrino/Guild Prototype Autogun Turret

During the First Spice War, Imperial and Guild engineers were working together to develop an improved Turret, with a high rate of fire, to improve their territorial defences and surpass the primitive Gun Turrets the rest of the Imperium uses and that are the current standard. Although the Prototype Turrets have never been finished,  during the aftermath of the war they began to be used to defend whatever was left of the Sardaukar and Guild territories. However, due to not being complete, their projectiles aren't very useful against armored vehicles. They are eating through infantry, though.
