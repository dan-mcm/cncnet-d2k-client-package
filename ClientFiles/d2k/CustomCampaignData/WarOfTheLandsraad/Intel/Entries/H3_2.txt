Harkonnen Heavy Tank

This is the backbone of the Harkonnen army. The Heavy Tank is the most resistent out of all three types of Combat Tanks, and is the main assault unit of the Harkonnen. Massive numbers of this tank can render enemies into scrap metal without much opposition, as they can take a lot of damage before going down. Raining down Heavy Tanks upon their enemies was a very viable Harkonnen tactic during the First Spice War.
