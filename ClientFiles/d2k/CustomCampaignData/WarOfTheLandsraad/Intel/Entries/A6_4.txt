Atreides Cruiser

The Atreides Cruiser is House Atreides' heavy bombardment ship. It is heavily armored and can bombard enemy position from a very far distance. There is no way to stop an Atreides Cruiser other than by attacking with your own navy or by air. During the invasion of Caladan, the Harkonnen took advantage of Atreides ships and used captured ships to fight the Atreides fleet. They had to pick small fights due to the massive size of the Atreides Navy.
