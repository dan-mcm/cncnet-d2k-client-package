Leto's influence within the Imperium and with Irulan Corrino

Duke Leto's influence within the Landsraad and the Imperium as a whole has grown a lot in recent years. His careful diplomacy, along with long-lasting partnerships and alliances based on trust and friendship, such as the alliance with House Vernius of Ix, have given Leto a lot of power. His influenced stretched even to the Corrino family, as Shaddam's daughter, Irulan, respects Leto a lot. They have met on multiple occassions and feasts, and have developed a pretty close relationship.
