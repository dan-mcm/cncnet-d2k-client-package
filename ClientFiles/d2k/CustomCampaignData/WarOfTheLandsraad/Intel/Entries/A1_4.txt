Kaitain

Kaitain is the capital-planet of House Corrino. The planet became the capital when the Imperial Seat was moved from Salusa Secundus—Salusa Secundus having become a wasteland after the intended destruction of House Corrino by House Tantor using atomics. That planet subsequently became the Emperor's prison planet. Kaitain is renowned for the amazing infrastructure and wonderful buildings, especially in the capital, Corrinth City.
