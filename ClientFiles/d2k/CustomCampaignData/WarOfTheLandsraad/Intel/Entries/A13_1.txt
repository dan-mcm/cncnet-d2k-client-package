Tleilaxu artificial spice melange

Created based on dead worm parts, combined with special Richesian technology, the Tleilaxu artificial spice is supposed to be a replacement for the natural spice of Arrakis, which is created by a worm when dying. The artificial spice is supposed to be of the same quality, but due to the Richesian special machines, it can be regenerated on the spot, making it a very powerful resource with never-ending potential. Or at least until it can be fueled by the worm parts and the Richesian machines.
