House Vernius of Ix

House Vernius is one of the most powerful Houses in the Landsraad, and the unchallanged technological leader. Their wealth comes from selling various ixian technologies and machines. Their technology can sometimes be too advanced, getting close to the boundaries enforced by the Butlerian Jihad in regards to thinking machines. Count Dominic Vernius is a very close friend of Leto Atreides, therefore there is a long-lasting alliance between Houses Atreides and Vernius.
