Duke Leto Atreides

Leto has inherited House Atreides from his father, Paulus Atreides. Leto is a very good strategist, and has developed a good reputation based on his honour. Members of the Landsraad consider Leto a man they can trust, so if they need help they would often seek the Atreides, as they are sure no scheming and hidden motives are involved on their side. Leto has also developed his diplomatic side, and is playing a diplomatic game, trying to secure alliances and form frienships or partnerships, rather than gain power by pure military power. Leto is surrounded by many honourable and loyal elite people, such as Gurney Halleck, Thufir Hawat and Duncan Idaho. This makes House Atreides a very powerful force, as inside plots of civil wars are almost non-existent.
