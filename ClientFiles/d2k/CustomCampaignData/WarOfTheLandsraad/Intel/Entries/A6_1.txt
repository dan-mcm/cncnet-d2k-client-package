House Moritani

The Moritani family is a pretty powerful member of the Landsraad, who have a long-lasting feud with House Ginaz. The conflict comes from the fact that Moritani has opposed for House Ginaz getting the title of a "Major House" in the Landsraad. The Moritani were victorious in a war against the Ginaz, but another war was never declared, not because the Moritani couldn't handle it, but because of the new alliance that Ginaz signed with House Atreides. This made House Moritani seek powerful allies themselves, which is the main reason why the Harkonnen-Moritani alliance was created many years ago. Even so, House Moritani did not declare war on House Ginaz anymore after getting their alliances.
