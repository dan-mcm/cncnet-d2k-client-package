Mutelli-Moritani issues

The fact that baron Vladimir Harkonnen has returned to Giedi Prime in the middle of an invasion, combined with the recent defeats on the southern continent and the loss of Serena's Necklace, which allows full Atreides naval retaliation, Houses Mutelli and Moritani have become quite nervous, mostly blaming each other for the poor performance. Feyd and Radnor had to manage this situation the best they could, along with some support from the Baron from Giedi Prime.
