The situation of the Arrakis Mercenaries after the First Spice War

During the First Spice War, most of the mercenary companies on Arrakis were destroyed by House Ordos, after being formerly employed by them. However, a few of them survived, and their leaders would rise up and unite some of the dispersed remnants of falled companies under their own banner. Even after the First Spice War, mercenaries were still in demand between Smuggler organizations.
