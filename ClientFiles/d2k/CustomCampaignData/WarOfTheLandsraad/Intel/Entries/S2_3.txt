Hagga Basin

Hagga Basin has a relatively massive and important Imperial stronghold on Arrakis. The stronghold was one of the first few Imperial bases that were set up by Emperor Shaddam IV, when he secretly moved his Sardaukar legions to Arrakis, with the help of House Harkonnen, during the initial phases of the First Spice War. After the fall of the Imperial Palace, the Hagga Basin stronghold served as a decoy for the Landsraad coalition. It was eventually overrun and occupied by the Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos. It is said that the Sardaukar forces stationed there fought bravely till the end, and the battle resulted with no prisoners due to the honour-bound fanaticism of the Sardaukars.
