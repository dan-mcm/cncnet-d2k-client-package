Zed Varlaz's war journal - Hijacking a Devastator

"We were forced to find a way to leave the Imperial base. Or whatever was left of it. We were hunted by the Landsraad, more specifically the Harkonnen. We saw a malfunctioning Devastator. They had to stop and fix it, and the rest of the army kept moving on. We waited, hidden behind rocks, for about an hour, until they had it fixed. Once they were getting ready to jump in and catch up with the Harkonnen army, we moved in and killed them. Then we took the Devastator. How many men can that thing fit? Well, let's say we gave it a complete new meaning that day... when Harkonnen scum are breathing down your neck waiting to drink your blood, you do whatever you have to survive."
