"Maybe he's a better baron..."

Extracted from Gato Radnor's personal journal: "I always thought Rabban is a complete joke, and not fit to be the future Baron. But I never believed Feyd would be much better. Until I got to fight an invasion with the kid. He is cunning, smart, a true tactician, and he can do all these while spending several nights with whores. I just got a sudden and overwhelming wave of admiration for this man. Maybe he's a better baron, after all..."
