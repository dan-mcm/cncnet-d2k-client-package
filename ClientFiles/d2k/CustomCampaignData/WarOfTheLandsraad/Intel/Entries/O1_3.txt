The Executrix of House Ordos

The Executrix is, actually, a union of four beings sharing a single mind, for efficiency. They communicate through an entity known as "the speaker", who talks in many voices, although they are always reffered to as the Executrix. The Executrix council and the speaker rule the Ordos with cold precision and authority; they lack most emotions. There is no word for loyalty in the Ordos vocabulary: there are over three hundred, however, for the concept of profit. The Executrix's only target is wealth and power through wealth: profit is all that is important, no matter the human cost.
