"The day a Sardaukar saved my life"

Extracted from an Atreides soldier's journal: "I never thought the day when I fight side by side with the Sardaukar would ever come, let alone one to save my life. Our tank was blown up, and I was standing there, wounded, with my tank colleagues dead, getting my final prayers going and ready to scream <For the Duke!> one last time. Then, Sardaukars would appear out of nowhere, making short work of the Harkonnen and Moritani soldiers threatening me. Then, one of them would offer his hand to help me get up. I couldn't believe my eyes!"
