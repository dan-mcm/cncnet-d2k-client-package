"Aim towards the Guild, Sardaukar!"

Extracted from a Sardaukar war journal: "We were standing there, confused, not knowing what to do. We were used to fighting with Guild troops side by side. Now we were presumably waiting to take them out, acting like we're cool. There was a cold silence going on. We, then, heared the order through radio comms: <Aim towards the Guild, Sardaukar! Leave no one standing. Move!>".
