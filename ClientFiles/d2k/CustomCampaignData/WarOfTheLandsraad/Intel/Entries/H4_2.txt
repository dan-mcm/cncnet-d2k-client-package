Rabban Harkonnen

Rabban is Vladimir's eldest son, and the heir to Giedi Prime. Although he is the oldest, Rabban is often viewed as a midless brute who is only interested in torturing slaves and killing everything he can. He lacks important assets for a future baron, such as a tactical mindset or the famous Harkonnen cunning plot ability. He is also a much weaker fighter compared to his brother Feyd, and is often made fun of because of all these.
