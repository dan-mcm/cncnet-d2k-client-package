Sabre Durant

Durant is the leader of a small mercenary company formed during the beginning of the First Spice War. After the destruction of most mercenary companies by the Ordos during the war, Durant would step up and unite a considerable amount of scattered mercenaries, members of the now-destroyed companies, under his own group. Although initially his squad was small, he was a very efficient battlefield commander, and this helped him to rally the surviving mercenaries under his own command. Now Sabre Durant commands the biggest and most efficient Mercenary company on Arrakis. He is an old friend of Ordos mentat Lora Itami. There are rumous of a potential old love relationship between the two in the past.
