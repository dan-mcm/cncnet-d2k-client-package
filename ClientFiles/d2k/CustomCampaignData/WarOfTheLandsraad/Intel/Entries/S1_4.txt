The Fall of the Imperial Palace

Battlefield journal entry from a Sardaukar soldier - "We were surrounded... We thought the Guild can hold it to the east, but they couldn't. We thought the geographical choke point offered by the rocks to the south can hold it... We though the Palace numerous defences can hold it... Then we saw the swarms of Atreides, Harkonnen and Ordos forces moving in for the Palace. We realized it was too late. They blew up the defences, and then... I don't even know what happened, I was knocked out. Some say it was a Death Hand, others say it was artillery bombardment... But the Palace collapsed. And so has emperor Shaddam IV."
