House Mutelli cease-fire request, for Duke Leto Atreides

"To Duke Leto Atreides, this is the head of House Mutelli. We are asking for a cease fire treaty between our House and yours and your allies. We have no further intention to fight the Atreides and their allies. We have been betrayed, and are asking House Atreides to forgive our hostilities. We shall concede defeat in regards to House Atreides and their allies, and will agree to the peace terms imposed by you, Duke Leto Atreides. This is an unconditional surrender statement. We now have a common enemy!"
