Atreides Medium Tank

The Medium Tank is a compromise between the Ordos speed and the Harkonnen armor. While it fits well in both categories, it does not excel in any. That does not mean the Medium Tank is not a strong asset: it is a very important vehicle in the Atreides arsenal, and is almost always the main unit used to push enemy lines, supported by Siege Tanks, Missile Tanks and Sonic Tanks from behind.
