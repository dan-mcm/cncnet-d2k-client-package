Rob Halvard

Halvard is a reputed smuggler leader on Arrakis. He is the head of one of the largest smuggler organizations. His most notable actions are illegal transportation of troops via owned heighliners based on Ixian technology, which do not require a Guild navigatior, but are less efficient. Besides transporting troops for Houses that pay, he has also shown his battle skills in the First Spice War, by joining forces with Atreides and Harkonnen in a battle where the two Great Houses destroyed an Imperial Jamming Facility which was disrupting Halvard's business.
