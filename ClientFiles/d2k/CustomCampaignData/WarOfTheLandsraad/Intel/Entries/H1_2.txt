Baron Vladimir Harkonnen

The ruthless Baron Harkonnen. He is one of the most powerful members of the Landsraad, and was reputed to have the strongest military in the entire Landsraad (excluding the Corrino Sardaukar, but the Corrinos are not a direct member of the Landsraad but actually the ruling House). This statement faded out after the First Spice War, as the Harkonnen army has suffered heavy casualties. Vladimir is a very ambitious man, and there is almost no room for mercy in his vocabulary. His ruthless aggression was proven effective, however, and his plots are one of the finest.
