House Bromeli:
The Bromeli were always obsessed with being part of the House of Ix or any other House related to technology. So much so that they came to develop a bomb powerful enough to knock down entire buildings.

However, the Ixians had just developed a similar technology but capable of being used thousands of miles away, so they quickly lost interest in Bromeli's invention and, without any army of their own to engage with, this lesser House can't even use their own invention.
