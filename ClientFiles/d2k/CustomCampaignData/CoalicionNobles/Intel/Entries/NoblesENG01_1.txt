House Lassoki:
Books contain little information about the Lesser House of Lassoki. Some historians claim that his army was a specialist in open combat while others deny this theory and that they were experts in hit-and-run tactics instead.

Although the origins of the formation of the Coalition are uncertain, there is a consensus that the Lassoki had a more prepared army than that of their three companions, so it has been concluded that they were the founders and leaders of the Coalition.
