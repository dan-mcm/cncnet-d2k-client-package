Unlimited Spice:
The Sandworms of Arrakis are respected and admired in equal measure for being natural producers of Melange Spice: the most valuable resource in the entire known universe. An area with multiple worms will have spice to sustain a battle for several hours.

During the war the Emperor had under his control Sandworms locked away producing Spice indefinitely. None of the Coalition members know of the method used to create this Sandworms farm, but the Bromeli have managed to imitate, albeit inefficiently, part of one of their farms. Although inefficient, the spice never runs out.
