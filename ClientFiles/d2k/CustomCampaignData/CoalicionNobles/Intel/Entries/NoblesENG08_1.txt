Death Hand Missile:
Equipped with highly volatile explosives; A single spark is enough to generate a chain combustion causing destruction in a very wide range, capable of destroying structures and groups of vehicles or, at least seriously damaging them.

The Bromeli have equipped this explosive in missiles that can be launched at great distances without the possibility of any countermeasures. Although it takes considerable time to assemble a large number of them, the Bromeli are true specialists in the field and know how to do it efficiently.
