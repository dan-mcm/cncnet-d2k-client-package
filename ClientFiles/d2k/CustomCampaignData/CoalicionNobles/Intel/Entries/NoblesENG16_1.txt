Sardaukar Elite:
The Sardaukar are soldiers recruited by the Emperor who survived on the prison planet: Salusa Secundus. This inhospitable place turned inmates into veritable killing machines; for this reason they are feared throughout the known universe.

That a Sardaukar equals 10 men is not just a rumor. The Sardaukar elite that make up the Emperor's personal guard are equipped with a gatling gun with a rate of fire capable of destroying most vehicles quickly, and their armor gives them extra protection from enemy bullets. These soldiers are very dangerous.
