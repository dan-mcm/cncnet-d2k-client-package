Sardaukar Ghola:
The Iasi count in their ranks the Sardaukar armies they longed for. However, these are not authentic Sardaukar but rather Gholas (human beings developed in Axlotl Tanks from the cells of dead individuals). The Tleilaxu have imbued these Sardaukar Ghola with even less fear of death than the originals, causing them to carry an inordinate number of body-mounted anti-tank mines that will seriously damage any vehicle trying to crush them.
