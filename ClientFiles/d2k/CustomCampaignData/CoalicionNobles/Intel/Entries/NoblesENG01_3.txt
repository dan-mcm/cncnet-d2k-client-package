House Qaii:
Isolated from much of the known universe, the Qaii were rarely inspected by the Emperor or any other influential group, which they took advantage of to create their own mechanical army.

The downside of this separation is that the Qaii were never able to buy high-quality materials from any group of Smugglers and had to settle for materials from their own planet, which has only allowed them to manufacture light vehicles equipped with small-caliber weapons.
