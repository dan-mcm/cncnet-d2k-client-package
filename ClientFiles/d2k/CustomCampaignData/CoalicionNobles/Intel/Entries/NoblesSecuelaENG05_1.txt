House Iasi:
The House Iasi of Nobles field a highly disciplined and battle-trained infantry army in their quest to equal the Imperial elite. After the victory of the War, the Iasi became a Major House, with all the benefits that this entails.

The specialty of the Iasi is the Armed Infantry, for this reason the Iasi elite is made up of Light Infantry, Soldiers and Grenadiers. Now that their resources are much higher, the Iasi can fight any armed group that turns against them.
