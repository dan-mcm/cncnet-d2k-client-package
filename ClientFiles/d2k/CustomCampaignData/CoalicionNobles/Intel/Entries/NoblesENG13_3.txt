Industrial waste:
Giedi Prime is nothing more than a planet-sized Factory. The Harkonnen produce and burn whatever they can in order to create the most powerful and feared army in the entire known universe. It is no surprise that no natural resources are found.

All that is available is waste from the various factories. Although they can be collected and recycled to be reused, their value decreases, requiring large amounts of them. It is worth 550 credits per full Harvester.
