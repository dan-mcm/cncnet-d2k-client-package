Coalition of Nobles:
The Coalition is made up of the 4 Noble houses of the Lassoki, Iasi, Qaii and Bromeli. These four groups control dune after the great war against the 3 main houses of the Atreides, Harkonnen, Ordos, including the Emperor himself. Each one controls their portion of Dune but they still maintain ties between them to react in case of any threat or invasion from third parties.
