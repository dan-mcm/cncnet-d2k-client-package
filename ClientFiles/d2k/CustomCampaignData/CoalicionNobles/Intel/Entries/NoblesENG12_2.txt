Qaii Elite:
The Qaii's specialty is fast Vehicles, so their most efficient army consists of Trikes, Quads and Raiders equipped with Ixian weapons. Additional buildings will be constructed to support hit-and-run and guerilla tactics.

Available buildings: Windtraps, Light Factory, Refinery, Silo, Outpost, Repair Center, High-Tech Factory and Ix Research Center, necessary to equip these special Raiders.
