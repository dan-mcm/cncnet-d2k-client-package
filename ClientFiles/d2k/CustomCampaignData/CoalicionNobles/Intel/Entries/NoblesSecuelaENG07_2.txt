Airstrike:
Ornithopters are light flying machines capable of equipping themselves with bombs or missiles, but due to their fragile structure they are often destroyed along the way or even when they are already above enemy defenses.

The Bromeli, however, were cunning enough to add countermeasures in the form of small flames that will deflect the only weapon capable of destroying them: guided missiles. Thanks to this ruse, the Bromeli Ornithopter successfully shot down remains to be seen.
