Bromeli bombings:
The Bromeli's specialty is explosives, so their most powerful weaponry is made up of the Ornithopters, the Death Hand Missile and the Saboteurs. Thanks to the acquisition of the Starport they can command their own army of vehicles, although it is somewhat slow to do.

Available buildings: Windtraps, Walls, Refinery, Silo, Modified Outpost, Repair Center, Atreides High-Tech Factory, Ix Research Center, Starport, Barracks and Missile Launcher.
