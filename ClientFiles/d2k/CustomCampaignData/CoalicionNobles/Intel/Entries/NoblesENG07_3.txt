Deviators:
Draconis IV, the home planet of the House of Ordos, is known to be a poorly habitable place due to its inhospitable conditions. One of the elements that makes it so unwelcoming are the silicon sediments that are scattered throughout the planet.

The Ixians thought experimentally with Silicon and, mixing it with other chemical elements, created a nerve gas that causes hallucinations if it is fired at vehicles. Using the model of the Missile Tank, the Ixians developed a variant equipped with nerve gas instead of explosives.
