Fedaykin Ghola - Infantry
     A rare breed of the fiercest warriors in the Known Universe, Fremen Fedaykin are death commandos with superior abilities to their Fremen Warrior counterparts. They are masters of guerilla warfare on Arrakis, making them impossible to see until they attack or get too close to your infantry. They also are hardier and have a faster rate of fire.

     The Tleilaxu have managed to create gholas of these legendary combatants. Your Fremen allies have followed Summers to Tsimpo Heights, so you have no means to match the enemy in stealth capability. However, Sardaukar Elites will fight for you. You must upgrade both your Barracks and High Tech Factory twice to build an Imperial Palace and make Sardaukar Elites available.
