Quad - LAV
     Rocket-toting LAVs ideal for surgical strikes against vehicles or structures. Despite their fragility, their speed and firepower are essential components of your military strength. Slower enemy vehicles on the open dunes are the natural prey of roving Quads.

     Enemy Quads are considered priority targets due to their tremendous attack power and low durability. Troopers or your own Quads can very easily annihilate enemy Quads. Your minigun in burstfire mode can shred Quads as well as any other vehicle.

     It's inadvisable to venture into the Sietch network with LAVs like Quads. Infantry would fare best in such tight spaces.
