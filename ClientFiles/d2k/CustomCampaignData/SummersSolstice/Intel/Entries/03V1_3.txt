Cyril Vasiliou - Commander
     The honorable Atreides commander. Atreides infantry are fiercely loyal to their duke, and the commander is no exception. House Atreides is attempting to ally with the native Fremen, but must rely only on their own forces until they've earned their trust. While respectable, do not mistake honor for kindness. The Atreides will fiercely and violently oppose their enemies.

     The proud Atreides think themselves above you. They're currently attacking your mining base at Bled al-Hazrad either to pillage Spice for their war chest, earn favor with the Emperor, or both. The Atreides may die for their Duke, but your men are loyal only to themselves. Your prosperity as a smuggler is intrinsic of your independence! Concessions would be utterly foolish.
