Siege Tank - LRA
     A cannon on treads capable of annihilating huge packs of infantry or most enemy structures in very few blasts. They excel at razing a base and its defenses, or at defending your own base from incoming enemy forces. They are also one of the best common weapons for blowing through Concrete Walls, outclassed only by rare units, such as Sonic Tanks.

     Siege Tanks are fairly cumbersome and can be destroyed easily by Quads and Combat Tanks. They should be kept behind your frontline units or fortifications for the best results. Once your local Heavy Factory is upgraded and a High Tech Factory is built, Siege Tanks can be manufactured. More can be ordered at a Starport.
