Tyrant Tank - Armor
     A colossal vehicle that once represented Imperial supremacy. Now, only the hubris that was the Emperor's downfall. Tyrant Tanks are infamously durable, and just as powerful. Today, they will be redeemed, become known for toppling a tyrant.

     The massive cannon mounted to the frame can obliterate any target on the ground quickly. Once your Heavy Factory is upgraded twice, and an Imperial Palace is present in the territory, you can deploy Tyrant Tanks to the field. Provided, of course, there is an empty OSO Heavy Factory for them to emerge from. Between these units and the hulking power armor and burstfire miniguns of Sardaukar Elites, your forces have never before held such a commanding presence. Press on, to final victory!
