Sabre Durant - Commander
     The resilient mercenary commander. Through his strong relationships with both you and Ketua Sumadi, he's gained much popularity and guaranteed relatively safe work for the many under his command. However, he is largely dependent on Sumadi's smugglers to operate and will do almost anything to maintain business with Sumadi.

     You and Durant have helped the likes of Sumadi and Aurelianus against overwhelming odds together before. Durant is known to put his hefty fees towards the toughest units available. His mercenaries are formidable and well worth the price.
