Rocket Turret - Base Defense
     Rotating missile platforms best fortified behind Concrete Walls and supported by friendly forces. They are effective tank snipers with an incredibly long range and perfectly precise attacks. Beware these towers! There's no greater threat to your vehicles when attacking an enemy base than Rocket Turrets.

     Rocket Turrets pivot much more slowly than Autogun Turrets. They are expensive and ineffective against scattered infantry. Their utter immobility leaves them vulnerable to a concentrated attack, and their power can be cut by destroying enough Wind Traps. Don't engage enemy defenders near Rocket Turrets, and use Grenadiers to clear out any walls protecting them.
