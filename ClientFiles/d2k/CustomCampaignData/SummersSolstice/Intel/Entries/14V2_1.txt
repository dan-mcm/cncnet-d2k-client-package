Fremen Ghola - Infantry
     Fremen Warriors are fierce combatants scattered about Dune. Quick, tough, and even stealthy at times, they were especially formidable when armed and organized by the Atreides. They proved equally effective against infantry, vehicles, and structures, and fought with ferocity that paralleled that of the Sardaukar. Against all odds, they were defeated by House Ordos and its allies.

     The Fremen Gholas you must rescue from the clutches of the Ordos are no mere imitations. They are greater, as they use their impressive combat prowess according to the will of the Mahai.
