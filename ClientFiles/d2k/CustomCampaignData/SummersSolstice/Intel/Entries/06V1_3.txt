Deviator - LRA
     Assuming Commanders Summers and Durant break the Imperial blockade on the Heighliner, the prototype weapon that will soon arrive at the smugglers' Starport is missile-launching LRA of Ixian design. The Deviator launches a sort of nerve gas in a highly pressurized canister that makes its victims quite suggestible. The gas only works in the confines of a vehicle, but infantry can be killed by the concussive force of a Deviator missile's detonation.

     The Atreides and Harkonnen use standard Missile Tanks. They have the same impressive range as Deviators, but launch explosive warheads which can be more precisely targeted even at long range. Raiders are effective against Missile Tanks.
