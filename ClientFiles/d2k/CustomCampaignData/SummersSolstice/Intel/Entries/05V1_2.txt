Harkonnen Heavy Combat Tank - Armor
     Harkonnen industry is undeniably powerful. Since they favor strength, the heavy Combat Tank is their choice in MBT.

     Heavy Combat Tanks have inferior speed and firepower to other Combat Tank variants, but their armor is extremely thick. They provide the perfect cover for the fragile, yet harder-hitting LAVs. Out-maneuver these vehicles and focus on the easier targets.

     Several sand mines have been laid to the north of your base to deter initial attacks from Harkonnen armor. They're a rarely used weapon due to being equally dangerous to your own forces. Avoid tripping them while you scout Gara Kulon.
