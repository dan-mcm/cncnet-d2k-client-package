Kailea Aurelianus - Commander
     The helpful Ixian commander. She is chiefly a scientist credited with overseeing the production of some incredible technology that debuted during the war for Arrakis. Summers' smugglers, your mercenaries, the Spacing Guild, CHOAM company, and other independent factions of less importance were responsible for its safe and swift distribution.

     Her skills are needed now more than ever as a special weapon must be devised and created to slay the sleeping Emperor Worm, a weapon already being developed here in Wind Pass. Her research must be protected at any cost!
