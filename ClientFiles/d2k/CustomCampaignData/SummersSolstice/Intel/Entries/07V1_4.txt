Ketua Sumadi - Commander
     The stealthy head of the largest smuggling organization on Arrakis. Ketua Sumadi has impressively turned a profit time and time again even as superior forces hunt his men down. He is known to micromanage his subordinates to propagate his modus operandi and ensure profits are made. And if that doesn't work, he will silence the opposition. This has angered some of his even more ambitious underlings, including yourself, who see potential in dealing with the Great Houses.

     Sumadi is not a skilled military commander, but his organization has attracted a large number of recruits. His center of operations on Arrakis is well-defended. Each of his closest lieutenants commands his own base between yours and Sumadi's.
