Achen Moriaen - Commander
     The ingenious commander of the Dark Alliance. The cunning ghola took advantage of Emperor Corrino's challenge to weaken her own competition. She set the House she served up to fail by purposefully allying with factions only as agreeable as their pay, then wound you up and set you loose against one another. All too late, the monster was finally revealed for what it is...

     The chaos and destruction sewn by her brilliant tactics almost guaranteed the Dark Alliance a flawless victory. It may yet come if no equally talented commander can rise to the task and defeat her in these final hours.
