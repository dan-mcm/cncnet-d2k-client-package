Silo - Support Structure
     Silos are fire-resistant storage buildings used for a variety of things, from processed Spice to ammo and fuel. While the former variant might not be an important target, the latter is vital for you to look out for! Munitions Silos violently explode when destroyed, causing damage to nearby units and structures. You must be certain your units are as far away as possible before attacking them.

     The Imperial base on the southern end of Shield Wall is full of Munitions Silos. You can't allow the supplies within to go towards enemy reinforcements for the battle at Alazor Steppe. By focusing on the Munitions Silos, you can more quickly and easily raze this Imperial base. You should be ready to follow up with your units though; the Imperials will surely rebuild if given the chance.
