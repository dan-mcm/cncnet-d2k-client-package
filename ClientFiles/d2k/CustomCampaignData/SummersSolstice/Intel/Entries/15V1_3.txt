Autogun Turret - Base Defense
     Rotating dual guns best fortified behind Concrete Walls and supported by friendly forces. Autogun Turrets are cheap and sturdy, and can be rapidly erected in defense of a base. Like Concrete Walls, Autogun Turrets will block all projectiles except those launched over them, such as grenades or long-range missiles. Upgrading the local Construction Yards will provide access to Autogun Turrets in the territory. You may need them urgently to defend against Moriaen's initial attacks.

     While effective at suppressing most enemy infantry and LAVs, their utter immobility leaves them vulnerable to a concentrated attack. And, their power can be cut by destroying enough Wind Traps, although Autogun Turrets are very power-efficient.
