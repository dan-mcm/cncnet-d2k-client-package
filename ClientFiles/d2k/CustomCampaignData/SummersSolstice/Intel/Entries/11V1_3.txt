RPG Quad - LAV
     RPG Quads are an uncommon hybrid seen only in the early stages of conflict, if at all. Although rare and expensive, and no tougher than typical Quads, RPG Quads are remarkably versatile. Not only can they engage enemies both in close range and from afar, even over walls, but they also make excellent scouts. They can meet any threat on the ground with an appropriate counter.

     Their good range and speed make them especially effective rapid response units. However, if you keep them grouped with LRA, they can both assail enemies from afar and protect the dedicated artillery units where they're most vulnerable.
