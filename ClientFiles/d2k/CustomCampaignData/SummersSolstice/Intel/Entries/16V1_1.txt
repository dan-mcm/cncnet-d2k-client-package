Fremen - Infantry
     Desert natives. Fremen Warriors are vastly superior to other infantry and can even match Sardaukar legionnaires in combat.

     Following the destruction of several Sietches by the Dark Alliance, the remaining Fremen leaders have resorted to using abandoned Atreides technology to survive. Given their desperation, they may be convinced to fight for you in the battle at Hajj Plateau to slay the abomination the Tleilaxu have spawned while it still sleeps.

     Fremen Fedaykin are also on the battlefield. They are stealthy paragon infantry, stronger even than Fremen Warriors.
