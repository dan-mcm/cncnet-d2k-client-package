Sardaukar - Infantry
     Emperor Corrino's fearsome and innumerable soldier-fanatics. Sardaukar legionnaires are armed with various weapons, each very potent: Heavy burstfire rifles, burstfire miniguns, or gauss rifles. And, they can take the most punishment of any infantry on Dune. They are religiously devoted to the Golden Lion Throne and will carry out the Emperor's will to the very end.

     Common Sardaukar legionnaires require only two Barracks upgrades and a High Tech Factory to be sent into the field. Sardaukar Elites fight only when an Imperial Palace has been erected in the territory. They represent the pride of the Sardaukar; they are paragons even among the very best. The very best Imperial units are expected to be common this close to the Emperor.
