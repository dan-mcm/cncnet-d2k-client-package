RPG Quad - LAV
     An uncommon LAV. Their role in combat operations is typically filled by two separate units: Grenadiers and Quads. They're primarily used in the early stages of a conflict, if at all, for their versatility and scouting proficiency.

     You have need for versatile units to safeguard the fledgling creature entrusted to your care. Not only can RPG Quads engage enemies both in close range and from afar, even over walls, but they also make excellent scouts. Combined with their good range and speed, they're especially effective rapid response units. RPG Quads can be built once your local Light Factories are upgraded and a High Tech Factory is erected in the same territory.
