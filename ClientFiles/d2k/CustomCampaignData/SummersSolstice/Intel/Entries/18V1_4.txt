Emperor Worm - All-Powerful Abomination
     The Spice Melange is the most valuable substance in the Known Universe. The drug was the impetus for the boom in interstellar travel and grants a longer lifespan, greater vitality, and heightened awareness, to name a few things.

     Through combined Guild and Tleilaxu ingenuity, they have managed to breed a man-worm hybrid with unimaginable abilities. The Emperor Worm is a living god that would wield almighty power over all that exists, and the only reason you still breathe is that it is asleep. Within the next couple of hours, you must secure passage for the Ixian Godslayer through all that remains of the cabal Moriaen commands and kill the abomination! Failure is not an option! If it awakens, all is lost.
