Fremen - Infantry
     Desert natives. Fremen Warriors are vastly superior to other infantry and can even match Sardaukar legionnaires in combat.

     The Atreides have secured a solid alliance with the Fremen; thus, Fremen Warriors are deployed from their Barracks. Fremen Fedaykin can be prepared simultaneously. Against their numbers, massing anti-infantry weapons is recommended.

     Palaces of any sort require two High Tech Factory upgrades to construct. They are the pinnacle of your tech tree! Capturing an Atreides HTF would grant you access to Ornithopters and Palaces of Diplomacy, given you possess their other prerequisites.
