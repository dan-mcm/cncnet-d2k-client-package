Sardaukar Ghola - Infantry
     Sardaukar legionnaires were Emperor Corrino's fearsome soldier-fanatics. They were armed with heavy weapons equally effective against infantry, vehicles, and structures. They could withstand the most punishment of any infantry on Dune, and were so devoted that even following the destruction of the Golden Lion Throne, they continued to resist you.

     Although Sardaukar gholas are wretched Tleilaxu imitations, they're just as tough as the original Sardaukar legionnaires. Their fierce loyalty turned in the favor of the Tleilaxu makes their infantry force as formidable as the Imperials'.
