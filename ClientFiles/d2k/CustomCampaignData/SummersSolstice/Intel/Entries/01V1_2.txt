RPG Quad - LAV
     RPG Quads are uncommon hybrids equipped to meet any potential threat on the ground with an appropriate counter. Their respectable speed and incredible sight radius make them excellent scouts. They're only seen in the early stages of conflict, if at all, as their role in combat is typically filled by two separate units: The standard rocket Quad, and the Grenadier.

    Beware these units! Their long-range explosive attack is a serious threat to your infantry while their missile launchers can wreck Raiders in close range. However, they are no tougher than the average Quad. When no easier solutions are available, defeat them with overwhelming force. Large numbers of scattered Troopers would be your best bet against these units.
