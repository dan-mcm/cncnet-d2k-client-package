Infantry Rock - Terrain Structure
     Dune is an incredibly hostile environment between its constant sandstorms, hellish heat, and giant sandworms. There are few safeties afforded to anyone here, but one refuge is a sort of terrain that's come to be known as 'infantry rock.' Consisting of any sort of terrain rough enough to obstruct vehicles and sandworms, infantry may garrison infantry rock and fire upon nearby threats with partial impunity. They cannot be crushed or devoured while on infantry rock.

     Infantry rock doesn't provide total protection. Grenades or howitzers could flush your men out. Although many infantry formed up tightly benefit from stacked firepower, they're tougher to rout if spread out. Always keep replacements prepared nearby.
