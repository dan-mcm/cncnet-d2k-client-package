Tleilaxu Heavy Combat Tank - Armor
     Strangely, the Tleilaxu don't settle on only one sort of MBT. Heavy Combat Tanks have also been seen among their forces.

     These units sacrifice speed in both motion and firing for superior armor. Despite inferior firepower to other Combat Tank variants, their sturdiness is unmatched. Gunfire and grenades won't so much as dent them, and they can flatten squads of Troopers long before their rockets penetrate the tanks' armor.
